## Getting started and account help

Use the <a href={env.urls.tyr}>mobile assistant</a> to record games,
<a href={env.urls.sigrun}>ratings and statistics</a> to view results, and the
<a href={env.urls.forseti}>profile and administration panel</a> to manage your account and events.
The same account works across these services.

On this page: [event modes](#event-modes), [creating an account](#sign-up), [joining an event](#registration),
[your profile](#profile), [changing a password](#password), and [common questions](#questions).

<h3 id='event-modes'>Which event mode do I need?</h3>

An **event** has its own player list, rules and rating table. A **session** is a game at one table;
in tournament controls, it can also mean all tables playing together. A **round** in the controls is one hand.

- **Local rating (club event):** players start games themselves, choose opponents and initial seats,
  and record hands in the mobile assistant. Tables play independently, without a shared tournament timer.
  Administrators can add players as the club rating continues.
- **Tournament:** administrators prepare the player list and seating, start all tables with the timer,
  and approve completed sessions together. Seating can be random, Swiss, interval-based or predefined.
- **Online event:** results come from supported online platforms and replay logs. Ask the organizer
  which platform and accounts the event requires; this flow differs from recording a game at a physical table.

<h3 id='sign-up'>How do I create an account?</h3>

1. Open <a href={`${env.urls.forseti}/profile/signup`}>Register new account</a>.
2. Enter a working email address, a player title and a strong password. Use a recognizable name and surname
   as your player title so organizers can find the right person.
3. Read the privacy policy, accept its terms and press **Register new account**.
4. Open the verification email and follow its confirmation link. Then log in.
5. Check your <a href={`${env.urls.forseti}/profile/manage`}>profile</a> and tell the organizer your account is ready.

If the email does not arrive, check spam and the address you entered. Contact the service administrator if you
still cannot activate the account. If you already have an account, recover its password instead of creating
another one: duplicate accounts split your game history. Sign up before arriving at a tournament.

<h3 id='registration'>How do I join a club rating or tournament?</h3>

**Signing up creates a Pantheon account. Player registration adds that account to a particular event.**
Creating an account alone does not add you to a tournament or club player list.

Send the organizer your player title or **System ID**, shown in your profile. The organizer adds you through
**Manage players**. Then log in to the mobile assistant and select the event. Also follow any separate
sign-up or payment process announced by the organizer.

<h3 id='profile'>How do I manage my profile?</h3>

1. Log in to the <a href={env.urls.forseti}>profile and administration panel</a> and open
   <a href={`${env.urls.forseti}/profile/manage`}>your account</a>. The mobile assistant also has a personal area
   button in settings.
2. Update **Player title**, country, city and any optional contact or Tenhou information. Enter your public
   Tenhou username, never a login string.
3. To add an avatar, enable the avatar option and choose an image. Use a square image up to 256 KB;
   other shapes are cropped to the center square.
4. Press **Save changes** and wait for the saved confirmation.

Your email address is read-only here. Contact the chief administrator if it needs changing.
Account removal deletes account data; it is not needed to leave an event or change a password.

<h3 id='password'>How do I change or recover my password?</h3>

Use email recovery both for a forgotten password and to choose a new one.

1. Open <a href={`${env.urls.forseti}/profile/resetPassword`}>Recover password</a>, enter your account email and submit.
2. Follow the link in the recovery email.
3. Press **Reset my password** on the confirmation page. This starts the reset: your old password stops working,
   so complete the next step before leaving the page.
4. Enter a new strong password and press **Update password**.
5. After the success message, go to the login page and use your new password.

If the link has expired or was already used, request another one. Check spam if the message is missing.

<h3 id='questions'>Common questions</h3>

**My event is missing in the assistant.** Ask the organizer to check that your exact account is registered
in the active event. Refresh the event list or select the event again in settings.

**The organizer cannot find me.** Save a recognizable player title, or give them your System ID.
Do not create another account to solve a search problem.

**Where are my results?** Open <a href={env.urls.sigrun}>ratings and statistics</a> and select the event.
Tournament results appear after administrator approval, subject to event visibility settings.
